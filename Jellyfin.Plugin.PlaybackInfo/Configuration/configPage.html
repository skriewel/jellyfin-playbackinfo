Playback Info

Shows video quality and play method in the web player while the playback controls are visible. Changes apply after reloading the web client (Ctrl+F5).

Content

Resolution and play method (Direct Play, Direct Stream, Transcoding) are always shown. Hovering the line shows all details.

e.g. HEVC 10-bit
HDR10, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, HLG. Nothing is shown for SDR.
The bitrate the player receives: the file's bitrate, or the target bitrate while transcoding.

Transcoding

e.g. "4K HEVC → 1080p H.264"
